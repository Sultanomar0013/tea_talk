Our Story

A little corner of tea culture, right next to São Bento.

Tea Talk brings the modern tea bar to Porto: drinks that start with properly brewed leaves, painted walls you'll want to photograph, and a team that's happy to walk you through your first bubble tea.

Grab a cup on the go, or sit down for a while and talk. That's what we're here for.
